# Hi, I'm Asish Ranjan Sahu
 
I'm a UX designer who builds what he designs. I care about how an interface feels in the hand: the sound of a keypress, the weight of a gesture, the space around a line of text. I prototype in code, so my ideas get tested instead of just drawn.
 
The question I ask before anything else: **does this need to exist?**
 
I'm currently looking for UX design roles and internships.
 
---
 
## How I design
 
- **Remove first.** I start by asking what can go. Most of my projects are defined by what they leave out.
- **Make it feel physical.** Sound, motion, and gesture tell people what is happening without a label.
- **Build it to test it.** I'd rather put a working prototype in someone's hands than defend a mockup.
- **Design for the person, not the metric.** I avoid noise, nagging, and tricks that pull attention. My tools work offline and keep your data on your device.

## Selected work
 
### Paper: a writing space that feels like paper
[View project](https://github.com/Sahu7337/Portfolio/tree/main/website/projects/Paper)
 
A distraction-free writing environment with synthesized mechanical-keyboard sounds, paper grain, and a folding-fan (*Sensu*) card deck that you navigate with trackpad gestures and a rotary mouse. The question behind it: can a screen make typing feel as good as a real desk?
 
`interaction design` `sound design` `gesture navigation` `javascript`
 
### Screened: a screenwriting tool with nothing in the way
[View project](https://github.com/Sahu7337/Portfolio/tree/main/website/projects/Screened)
 
Built around how screenwriters actually type, with industry-standard pagination and no visual clutter.
 
`typography` `minimal UI` `javascript`
 
### Joi: a voice-first companion
[View project](https://github.com/Sahu7337/Portfolio/tree/main/website/projects/Joi)
 
Inspired by *Blade Runner 2049*. I replaced chat bubbles with a dark screen and a softly breathing, audio-reactive core, so talking to it feels more like presence than a text thread. It runs on local models, so conversations stay private.
 
`voice UI` `motion` `python` `fastapi` `ollama`
 
### Sifter: making safety legible
My hackathon project (cybersecurity track). You paste in a link or message and get a trust score, so people without a security background can decide whether to click.
 
`security` `clear communication`
 
### Sentinel SOS: an emergency app with no room for confusion
An Android app that detects a hard impact, sends your location, and falls back to SMS. When someone is in trouble there's no time to learn an interface, so the design had to be instant. I had never built an Android app before this, and I shipped it.
 
`kotlin` `android` `sensors`
